## 3. Mixture, budget rule and the 48K record

Budget rule (plan section 4): the main SFT run must finish inside `max_hours` (plan: ~7.5 to 8 h) at the throughput measured at the
chosen cap (M4.1, T7). Every sequence token of the mixture (training rows and validation shard, as the plan's sizing
table counts them) is costed at that throughput. When the mixture does not fit, whole units are trimmed in this order:
1. Python SWE rows: SWE-rebench-V2 Python first, then Scale-SWE, each the last drawn first (the draw puts large tasks
   first, so the random-phase rows go first); Go / JS / TS rows are never trimmed;
2. retention rows, proportionally across its parts, down to a 12 percent loss-token floor;
3. whole terminal episodes, TerminalHorizon before TermGrade, proportionally across the category-quota groups.

## 9. Deduplication and decontamination

Screens of plan section 7, run by `scripts/data/run_screens.py` in two passes; counts only, never text. Pass `p1_pools` screens one item per pool task (the task text the rows contain) plus the frozen dev items, so train-vs-dev overlap is checked on text; pass `p2_rows` screens the rendered training and validation rows (decoded). A `p1_pools` drop removes every row of the task; a `p2_rows` drop removes the hit row's unit (an SWE row, a terminal episode with both trained turns, a retention row), the mixture is rebuilt and `p2_rows` runs again on the new files until it drops nothing. The screens never rewrite pool files. The D3 screen matches hashed word 8-/13-grams and bge-small embeddings against a sealed index kept outside the repository: any shared 13-gram, >= 50 percent of an item's 8-grams in one indexed item, or cosine >= 0.85 (task and prompt items only) drops the item. The screens catch close copies; they do not prove the absence of contamination.

## 12. Limitations

- Training tasks are easier than SWE-bench Pro HARD-51 by construction: HARD-51 is the hard subset of SWE-bench Pro (the public paper reports larger, multi-file patches than SWE-bench Verified), while every SWE row is a task a 27B teacher resolved inside the row cap (size-stratified toward multi-file changes, still smaller on average).
- Every loss-bearing token is off-policy teacher text (Qwen3.8-27B, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GPT-OSS-120B, GLM-5); the retention slice is ready-made, not generated by the student.
- Upstream labels are trusted: Open-SWE-Traces `resolved` (NVIDIA's execution label) and TermGrade `reward` (Harbor verifier). Science MCQ, Science RQA and chat rows are format-checked only (`label_provenance = format`).
- TermGrade tasks are synthetic and short-horizon; transfer to Terminal-Bench is measured on the dev terminal slice, not assumed.
- The screens catch verbatim, near-verbatim and close-paraphrase overlap (n-gram and embedding thresholds); they do not prove the absence of contamination.
- The validation shard is grouped by task; repositories may appear on both sides (it measures the loss curve, not generalisation).
